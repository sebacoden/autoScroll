AutoScroller automatically advances to the next video in your vertical feeds every N seconds without touching the screen.

Main features:

* Gesture activation: 3-finger tap or upward swipes within the configured app
* Smart pause: Manual touch or scroll suspends auto-scroll and resumes later
* Digital wellbeing: Background timer and notifications when you reach your time limit
* Privacy: 100% local. Uses AccessibilityService, no internet permission required
